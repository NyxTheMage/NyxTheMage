My Current Headspace

I like coding. I enjoy making stuff. I hate reviewing code and micromanaging. So I don't find much use in coding chatbots. The technology is impressive, but they're still only statistical models hallucinating an approximation of whatever the user prompts. Slop vending machines. I honestly feel like the AI-pilled might be in some kind of cult or mass delusion?

We built a tool and then society broke. - ex-ceo of Google, Eric Schmidt. This shithead had a hand in making the world these students now need to find work in. Why did he build all the dark patterns then? Did he just follow the money and just not think of the repercussions? Real "Millions of you may die, but that is a sacrifice I'm willing to make" energy.

Last few years listening to NVidia I have had moments where my brain is like "yay Star Wars future" but by the end of the conference I just feel dumber? The creative in me likes to play pretend, but the humanist in me who has to live in the real world just sees it all very dystopian?
